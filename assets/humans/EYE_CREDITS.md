The user-supplied `human_eye.glb` is preserved here. `human_eyes.npz` contains
reduced, reoriented meshes and the original embedded texture maps, fitted to
GAIA's proportion-corrected male, female and player heads. The globe/iris
dimensions were adjusted to adult scale. Procedural eyelids, blinking and gaze
motion were added. The authored corneal transmission mask reveals the inset
iris; GAIA approximates the wet corneal reflection without a refraction pass.

Rebuild with `python tools/bake_human_eyes.py` after changing the body asset.
